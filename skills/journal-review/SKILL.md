# Evidence review guide

Read `../../docs/approach.md` and `../../templates/journal-entry.md`, resolving them from the user-provided repository if installed separately. Ask for the location if unavailable.

## Workflow

1. Obtain the original entry and supplied evidence. Identify the advance hypothesis, primary metric, threshold, guardrails, design, collection window and any amendments. If these did not exist before results, label the review retrospective; do not manufacture a preregistration.
2. Check comparability before calculating: formulas, sources, scope, exposure, maturity, missingness, exclusions and case mix. Identify deviations and their consequences. If comparison is invalid, explain the specific break and what can still be reported.
3. Calculate original values, counts, absolute change, and appropriate relative change with a reproducible calculation trail. Verify arithmetic. For rates show numerators and denominators; distinguish percentage points from relative percentages. Zero baselines have no defined relative change.
4. Compare with the original prediction and practical target. Explicitly surface expectation gaps. Do not reinterpret “felt 30% better” as a measured claim unless a specific metric was defined. Do not replace a failed primary outcome with a favorable exploratory result.
5. Evaluate the design and alternative explanations. Before/after supports an observation, not isolated causal attribution. A comparison group needs defensible comparability and trend assumptions. Random assignment still requires checking execution, spillovers, missing data and analysis. Do not assign arbitrary confidence scores or manufacture confidence intervals, p-values or statistical power from insufficient data.
6. Report observed magnitude separately from precision, attribution and practical value. “Target missed” is not “no effect,” and “target met” is not “caused by the change.” Note when the evidence is inconclusive.
7. Recommend keep, adjust, roll back, or inconclusive/collect more with reasons, guardrail performance, costs, reversibility, owner and review trigger. A provisional keep is allowed without declaring success. Propose follow-up measurement as a new plan, not silently extending the original window until results look favorable.

## Output

Fill the results and decision sections while preserving the original plan. Lead with a plain-language finding, then the calculation table, expected-versus-observed comparison, data-quality findings, alternative explanations, evidence limits and decision. Separate facts, interpretations and proposed next actions. If evidence is missing, return a partial review and precise requests; never fill gaps with plausible numbers.
