## Comparison and alternative explanations

- **Design:** Before/after / comparison group / randomized trial
- **Why this design fits:** Decision stakes, feasibility and limits.
- **Comparison details:** Units, periods, group eligibility and selection; assignment method if randomized.
- **Assumptions and checks:** Prior trends, case mix, spillovers, concurrent changes, and exposure differences.
- **Alternative explanations:** What else could move the metric, what evidence to record, how to check it.
- **Permitted claim:** What could this design establish? What could it not?
- **Readiness gaps:** Missing information, responsible person and next action. Unknowns remain unknown.
